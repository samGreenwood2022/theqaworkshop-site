Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy TEST explains what personal information The QA Workshop collects, why, and what your rights are. I've tried to keep it short and in plain English, because there isn't much to it: this website doesn't have accounts, forms, advertising or analytics.

Who I am

The QA Workshop is operated by Sam Greenwood through Cruickshank Consultants Limited ("I", "me"). I'm the "data controller" for the information described below. You can contact me at sam@theqaworkshop.co.uk.

What this website collects

The website itself is a simple page. It doesn't use cookies, tracking, analytics or advertising, and it has no sign-up or contact forms. Two things are worth knowing:

  • Hosting. The site is delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address and browser type in order to serve the page and protect it from abuse.
  • Fonts. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address as a result.

When you get in touch

I only collect personal information if you choose to contact me or book a chat.

My lawful bases

  • Taking steps at your request or performing a contract, when you ask about or book the course.
  • Legitimate interests, in replying to enquiries and running a small business sensibly and securely.
  • Legal obligation, in keeping the financial records I'm required to keep.

Who I share it with

Only the services I use to run the business, who process information on my behalf: Calendly (bookings), Google (calendar and email), Cloudflare (website hosting and email routing), and my accountant where needed for my accounts. Some of these providers are based outside the UK. Where information is transferred internationally, I rely on the safeguards those providers put in place, such as the UK's approved transfer arrangements.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries and booking messages: for as long as needed to deal with your request, and for a reasonable period afterwards. If nothing comes of it, I'll delete them after about 12 months.
  • Invoices and financial records: for 6 years, as UK tax rules require.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask me to:

  • tell you what personal information I hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete your information (unless I need to keep it by law);
  • restrict or object to how I use it.

To do any of these, email sam@theqaworkshop.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. I'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Links to other sites

The page links to Calendly and LinkedIn. They have their own privacy policies, and I'm not responsible for how they handle your information.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle personal information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
